This is the conformance client for vitai, not a stats product: it proves the engine's read model is consumable by someone who is not the flagship app. Its one rule is that it may not compute - every number here came out of a table. Where a figure is missing, the engine does not emit it, and that is a finding rather than a gap in this page. See RULES.md.
